I. EWC & Continual Learning — Core Methods
[1]
Core method
Overcoming Catastrophic Forgetting in Neural Networks
Kirkpatrick, J., Pascanu, R., Rabinowitz, N., et al. (2017). Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS), 114(13), 3521–3526. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1611835114
https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00796 · https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1611835114
⚑ Primary EWC citation. Introduces the Fisher Information Matrix penalty to prevent catastrophic forgetting. Foundational to every EWC claim in your paper.
[2]
Core method
EWC Done Right for Continual Learning (EWC-DR)
Anonymous (2025). NeurIPS 2025 Workshop. arXiv:2603.18596
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.18596
Shows vanilla EWC achieves 90.66% forgetting rate on NLVR2; EWC-DR reduces it to 27.48%. Cite when discussing EWC limitations and potential improvements.
[3]
Core method
Elastic Weight Consolidation for Knowledge Graph Continual Learning: An Empirical Evaluation
Jhajj, G. & Lin, F. (2025). NeurIPS 2025 Workshop on Knowledge Graphs & Agentic Systems. arXiv:2512.01890
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01890
EWC reduces forgetting 12.62% → 6.85% (45.7% reduction, λ=10 optimal). Task construction strategy significantly affects forgetting magnitude. Directly supports your attack-ordering ablation (Plan B2).
[4]
Core method
Communication-Efficient Federated Continual Learning for Distributed Learning System with Non-IID Data (FedSI / CFedSI)
Zhang, Z., Zhang, Y., Guo, D., Zhao, S. & Zhu, X. (2023). Science China Information Sciences, 66(2), 122102.
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/366859580
Adapts Synaptic Intelligence (SI, same family as EWC) to FL on non-IID data. Proposes bidirectional compression (BCEC). Closest methodological precedent to your work outside the IDS domain.
[5]
Core method
Evaluating Differential Privacy in Federated Continual Learning: A Catastrophic Forgetting–Performance Tradeoff Analysis
Chen, C., Lian, Z., Su, C. & Sakurai, K. (2024). 12th Int. Symposium on Computing and Networking (CANDAR), IEEE, pp. 135–141.
Referenced in: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.13840
Quantifies the DP ε vs. forgetting vs. accuracy three-way trade-off in FCL. Directly underpins your Plan E2 (Opacus DP). Closest existing work to your DP research direction.
[6]
Core method
AFCL: Analytic Federated Continual Learning for Spatio-Temporal Invariance of Non-IID Data
Tang, J. et al. (2025). arXiv:2505.12245
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.12245
Addresses both spatial (non-IID across clients) and temporal (new tasks) catastrophic forgetting in FCL. Proposes gradient-free method. Motivates your non-IID client design and heterogeneous traffic experiment.
[7]
Core method
GFCL: A GRU-Based Federated Continual Learning Framework Against Data Poisoning Attacks in IoV
Talpur, A. & Gurusamy, M. (2022). arXiv:2204.11010
Referenced in: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.13840
Only prior FCL work that explicitly addresses data poisoning within the training loop. GRU for IoV, not IDS. Cite as security motivation for your poisoning simulation (Plan E1).
